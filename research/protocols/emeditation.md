# eMeditation — IDI / FGI protocol

Status: planned. Recruitment has not started. Guide prepared in October 2026.

## Question
Can someone set a duration and recognise progress without feeling judged?

## Recruitment
Adults with varied meditation experience, including people who paused a routine; include different vision and motor-access needs. Ask about app use, not medical diagnoses.

Start with a purposive pilot of 4–6 IDI participants per project, not a representative sample or a promise of saturation. Review missing perspectives and dialogue quality after the pilot, then revise recruitment. Do not pool unrelated projects into one interview population.

## IDI · 45–60 minutes
0–5 min: explain purpose, voluntary participation, separate recording permission and withdrawal arrangements. 5–15 min: recent experience. 15–40 min: artifact tasks with neutral probes. 40–50 min: reflection and counterexamples. Final minutes: questions and debrief. Pilot the guide before recruitment.

### Neutral questions
1. Walk me through the last time you tried a short practice. What happened before you started?
2. How would you choose how long to practise today?
3. What does this progress screen tell you? What does it leave unclear?
4. Imagine returning after two weeks away. How would this feedback feel?

### Tasks
1. Set the timer to eight minutes and thirty-five seconds, then point to Start.
2. Identify what to do in each breathing phase using words and numbers as well as colour.
3. Find a previous session and skip the optional mood check-in.

### Observation and evidence
Record selected versus intended duration, corrections, assistance, interpretation of totals and language used about returning after a break. A brief concept test cannot measure habit formation or wellbeing.
Record the exact task, artifact version, assistance, observable action and participant wording separately from interpretation. Do not convert planned measures into results. If showing alternatives, counterbalance order and record order effects. A small qualitative study explores mechanisms and problems; it cannot demonstrate a population-level causal effect.

## FGI · 60–75 minutes · optional complementary phase
Optional: one group of 4–6 adults discusses wording of completion and progress feedback after private ratings. Do not ask people to disclose personal health information. Keep individual comfort and accessibility tasks in IDI.
Agenda: 0–10 consent and ground rules; 10–20 private responses; 20–50 moderated discussion of artifacts; 50–65 disagreements and alternatives; 65–75 reflection and debrief. Invite quieter voices without forcing disclosure. Mark speaker IDs and moderator prompts. Code agreement, disagreement and changes after group discussion separately.

## Analysis plan
Import pseudonymised transcripts as new documents, kept apart from the case-study texts and design artefacts already in the project. Read each transcript in full. Start open coding before comparing with the design-review codebook. Revise definitions, add unexpected codes, log negative cases and write reflexive memos. Compare candidate patterns within each project before writing any cross-project synthesis. Keep artifact observation, participant report and analyst interpretation distinct. A code count is not importance, prevalence or proof of causality. No inter-coder reliability or saturation has been assessed.

## Consent script to adapt before recruitment
We are studying this design, not testing you. Taking part is voluntary; you can skip a question or stop. May I take notes? May I record this session separately? A refusal to recording does not prevent participation. Before starting, the researcher must name who can access the data, where it will be stored, the deletion date and the withdrawal deadline. Do not begin recording until these details are agreed. Request separate permission for any portfolio quotation or image. Group confidentiality cannot be guaranteed: ask everyone to avoid repeating others’ contributions. Use participant IDs, keep contact details separate and remove names from transcripts. Do not upload identifiable transcripts to third-party cloud services without appropriate permission.
