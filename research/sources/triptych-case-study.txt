WORLD · TRIPTYCH — CASE STUDY (PORTFOLIO TEXT)
Triptych · Digital collage · Procreate · 2022
Role: Artist · Technique: Digital collage, Procreate · Year: 2022 · Recognition: Prize, Nezalezhne Slovo, 2023

WHY I MADE THIS SERIES
World is a triptych I made at the start of Russia’s full-scale war against Ukraine in 2022. It reflects my response to the world’s reaction: realisation, a sense of betrayal and the wait for action. I wanted to give those feelings a visible form across three connected works.
This is a personal art series. The design decisions serve an emotional statement: the portraits, colour fragments and handwritten words work together to communicate the tension of that moment.

PHOTOS, COLOUR AND HANDWRITING
I built the compositions in Procreate using black-and-white stock photographs of people in traditional Ukrainian dress. The restrained photographs provide a common foundation; painted fragments, yellow outlines and handwriting interrupt it with colour and a more immediate, personal voice.
The repeated visual language holds the three panels together while allowing each composition to stand alone. Viewed as a group, the portraits and lettering become a continuous wall of images and statements. My role covered the concept, image selection, collage, colour treatment and handwritten elements.

EXHIBITION AND AWARD
World was shown at PRAY4UKRAINE, a charity exhibition that directs proceeds from sold works to the Armed Forces of Ukraine. The triptych also won a prize in the Fine Art category of the Nezalezhne Slovo (Independent Word) project. The award ceremony took place in Lviv on 1 June 2023.
The series shows how I use contrast, repetition and lettering to carry a message across several compositions. Presenting it outside the original digital format also brought the three panels together as a public statement.
