DESIGNING FOR KSI
Since July 2024, I have worked as a promotion ambassador and designer for KSI, the Computer Science Students’ Association at Jagiellonian University. I create event posters, graphics and T-shirt prints for a community that meets around both technical interests and social activities.
The KSI mark was the only fixed identity element at the start. My task was to build visual directions around it that could suit a machine learning workshop, a conference, a grill or a tournament. The challenge was to give each event its own character while keeping the association visible.

POSTERS FOR THE UNIVERSITY CORRIDORS
I designed the posters for a busy university corridor, where someone might only glance at the wall while rushing from one class to the next. Across the series, I chose bright, contrasting colours and large type to help each poster stand out in that brief moment. The hierarchy gives the event title a clear first read, followed by the details needed to take part.
I approached every poster with a distinct visual idea. The machine learning poster turns the word “model” into a spiral; the grill poster uses a halftone ξ. I wanted each new event to bring something fresh to the wall: a composition that catches attention on the way past and rewards a closer look. Making the posters visually appealing and interesting to encounter was part of the brief I set myself.
The concept and colour support a practical invitation: what is happening, when, where and how to take part. Every poster shown here was published. The originals are in Polish; I prepared the English versions for this portfolio.

THE PRINTED T-SHIRTS
For merchandise, I drew on programming, binary code and symbols familiar to KSI members. The binary tee pairs a small ξ on the chest with a field of zeros and ones on the back. The cobalt tee combines a pixel outline with a Polish manifesto about the community.
Both designs went to print. The gallery separates the artwork from lookbook mockups, which show how the binary design could be presented in a wider visual context.

OTHER T-SHIRT CONCEPTS
The unprinted concepts explore other ways to represent the group: a tour tee built around the year’s events, a field guide to the species of the KSI ecosystem, three icons for compile, meet and inspire, and a ξ assembled from keyboard keys.
Each direction starts from something members can recognise—shared events, habits or tools—and translates it into a graphic idea. These are concept explorations, shown separately from the two produced designs.

PUBLISHED AND PRINTED WORK
The published posters and two printed tees show how I adapt a limited starting identity to different messages and formats. My contribution spans visual concepts, typography, composition and artwork for posters and merchandise.
The outcome is a body of work used by the association, with a distinction between published posters, produced merchandise and exploratory concepts. Together, they show both the range of the visual direction and how it translates into finished pieces.
